# doom_neuralAI

## Description

The overall goal of this project is to see how limiting an models reaction time as well as the amount of actions it takes affects how it learns and the overall quality of the model. The overall goal is not necessarily to make it good at the game but to force it to approach it in a way a human would.

## Motivation

Often AI Models are created to just be really good at something and the evaluation of it is such. But the overall goal of AI at least in the context of Video games should be to emulate a person rather than surpass them. So Instead of focusing on the objective results of the model it would be more interesting to see how well it imitates a real person or how training in different ways effects the way it learns and the solutions it comes up with.

## Ideal Outcome

While I mentioned that the model doesn't necessarily need to be good at the game that doesn't mean it should be bad. Ideally the model should be decent enough to play against a new to average player while also using different approaches than really just becoming an aim bot at some point and watching it make mistakes. The overall goal however is still to see how it goes about reaching this goal.
